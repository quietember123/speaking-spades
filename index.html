Rules in this demo

Four players, fixed partners (You + Partner). Everyone bids 0–13 (0 = Nil). Follow the led suit when possible. Jokers count as spades in Joker mode. Spades cannot lead until broken unless your hand contains only trumps. Team contract: 10 points per bid trick, 1 per overtrick; missed contract: −10 per bid trick. Nil: +100 if successful, −100 if failed. Every 10 bags costs 100 points. First team to 500 after a hand wins (ties continue).

Practice only: computer players use simple strategy. No real accounts, online opponents, or voice chat yet. Audio begins after user interaction. Joker rules vary by region; this demo uses the rules stated above.
